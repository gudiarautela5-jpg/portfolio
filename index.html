About

I'm a third-year UI/UX design student at Manipal University Jaipur. I care about designing for people, not just screens. Good design comes from understanding everyday problems, asking the right questions, and finding simple yet meaningful ways to solve them.

I'm drawn to thoughtful details, clear communication, and experiences that feel effortless to use. For me, design isn't just about how something looks; it's about how it works, how it feels, and the difference it makes in someone's everyday life.
